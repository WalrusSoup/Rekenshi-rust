# Dust SSAO source investigation

Investigated 2026-10-08. Source pinned to [Dust commit `365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce`](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/tree/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce). Read-only source investigation; no game changes, shader compilation, in-game validation, or GPU measurements.

## Finding

Dust implements real screen-space geometry-based ambient occlusion and integrates it into Kenshi's lighting. The technique belongs to the familiar horizon-based AO family. The unusual engineering is the game-specific injection that makes Kenshi consume its AO texture during lighting. Its shader is labeled GTAO, but the inspected math is a custom simplification rather than the complete original GTAO analytic slice integration. This distinction is an inference from the code, not an upstream claim of compliance.

## Actual AO algorithm

- The active renderer compiles external `ssao_gen_ps.hlsl`, two blur shaders and a debug shader. The older embedded shader strings and the half-resolution upsample file do not establish the active pipeline. [Renderer initialization](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAORenderer.cpp#L130-L179)
- It reconstructs view positions from the game's depth texture, aspect ratio and camera field of view. It calculates a geometric normal using neighboring depths, selecting the neighbor with the smaller depth difference to reduce silhouette contamination. It also decodes the real G-buffer normal, transforms it through the camera basis, and blends the two normal estimates. The default normal-detail blend is 0.5. [Reconstruction and normals](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/shaders/ssao_gen_ps.hlsl#L42-L104), [defaults](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAOConfig.h#L8-L36)
- It searches both sides of several screen directions. Each sample produces `dot(normalized displacement, normal)`, multiplied by distance falloff and foreground rejection; it retains the maximum per side and averages those maxima into visibility. This is a horizon-style heuristic. It lacks the projected slice-normal calculation and analytic cosine-weighted slice integration that characterize full GTAO. Calling it a simplified horizon-based implementation is more precise than assuming the GTAO comment proves a faithful implementation. [Sampling and accumulation](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/shaders/ssao_gen_ps.hlsl#L106-L205)
- Default sampling is four directions with four steps in both directions: 32 search depth samples per pixel, in addition to depth/normal reconstruction. The renderer allows 4–12 directions and 2–6 steps. [Defaults](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAOConfig.h#L33-L36), [clamps](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAORenderer.cpp#L367-L398)
- Generation and filtering run at full resolution in two R8 render targets, with three passes: generation and two spatial, depth-guided 4×4 neighborhood filters. The blur filenames say horizontal/vertical, but both shaders use 2D gather neighborhoods. No temporal AO history or motion-vector reprojection is used in this path. The per-pixel noise rotation is spatially fixed. [Targets](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAORenderer.cpp#L116-L126), [passes](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAORenderer.cpp#L409-L465), [filter](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/shaders/ssao_blur_h_ps.hlsl#L31-L72)
- Foreground rejection exponentially suppresses contributions from samples much closer to the camera; radius clamping, invalid-depth rejection and distance fading further constrain the effect. These are practical halo safeguards, not proof that foliage and silhouettes are artifact-free. [Generation safeguards](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/shaders/ssao_gen_ps.hlsl#L109-L203)

## Kenshi integration

Dust identifies the lighting pass and retains its G-buffer resources: albedo at slot 0, normals at slot 1, depth at slot 2, plus the HDR target. Before the game's lighting draw, the SSAO plugin generates the AO texture and binds it for the patched game shader. The stage name `POST_LIGHTING` has both pre- and post-draw callbacks; AO generation is in the pre-draw callback. [Resource capture](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/src/PipelineDetector.cpp#L105-L139), [plugin callback](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/DustSSAO.cpp#L139-L220)

Dust intercepts `D3DCompile`, finds text anchors in Kenshi's deferred shader source, inserts AO sampler declarations and lighting multiplications, and compiles the modified source in memory. It falls back to the original compile path when patch compilation fails. This is source interception and recompilation, more specific than the README's broad description of bytecode patching. No persistent replacement of the game's shader files is required. [Deferred source patch](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/src/ShaderPatch.cpp#L92-L139), [compile interception](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/src/ShaderPatch.cpp#L900-L975)

The inserted code multiplies environment diffuse and specular lighting by AO before fog/tonemapping/UI. It can also attenuate sunlight and separately patched point/spot lights using `lerp(1, ao, directAO)`. **The source default for Direct Light AO is 0.3**, so the README's description of ambient-only AO does not match the default configuration. Setting this control to zero makes the SSAO contribution ambient-only. Applying isotropic AO to direct light is an artistic approximation, not directional visibility or a replacement for light shadows. [Lighting equations](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/src/ShaderPatch.cpp#L126-L135), [local lights](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/src/ShaderPatch.cpp#L306-L360), [default](https://github.com/Bazouz660/Dust/blob/365c903515d957904ee447605541005697cc5fce/effects/ssao/SSAOConfig.h#L30-L31)

## Implications for this project

The useful precedent is obtaining the actual geometry buffers and feeding AO into ambient lighting before fog, rather than multiplying the finished image. Dust demonstrates feasibility. It does not establish that its exact sampling, depth reconstruction, tuning, or hook detection will be correct in our backend without verification. A prototype should first validate depth/normal/camera reconstruction, then test subtle AO at Direct Light AO = 0, including foliage, camera motion, sky silhouettes, fog, interiors and local lights. Runtime measurements remain necessary.
